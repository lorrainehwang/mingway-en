Partnership

A partner in talent value, not a vendor of CVs.

Our business runs on a Key Account model. Through high-quality, high-efficiency talent connections, we earn long-term trust — and become a genuine partner in our clients' growth.

Our Vision

Serving from a growth perspective — a three-way win

Every engagement is built so that all three sides move forward together: the client, the candidate, and Mingway.

Ⅰ

For Clients

High-quality, high-efficiency talent connections that answer real business needs — today and as the organisation grows.

Ⅱ

For Candidates

Deep, professional service and a long-term relationship — we walk alongside outstanding people as their careers develop.

Ⅲ

For Mingway

Trust earned through delivery. Each successful mandate deepens the partnership — we grow as our clients and candidates grow.

Our Process

Disciplined, transparent, built for delivery

01

Briefing & Calibration

We go beyond the job description — understanding the role's mandate, its positioning, the team's goals and the company culture, so the search starts precisely aimed.

02

Market Mapping

We map the target talent pool across our practice industries, identifying where the right profiles sit and what will move them.

03

Approach & Assessment

Discreet, direct outreach. We assess not only capability and experience, but career aspirations and values — the ingredients of a lasting match.

04

Shortlist & Interviews

A tight, well-briefed shortlist — not a pile of CVs. We coordinate interviews and keep both sides aligned throughout.

05

Offer & Closing

From referencing to negotiation, we manage the delicate final stretch so the right offer lands — and is accepted.

06

Onboarding & Beyond

We stay close after day one. Long-term partnership means we care how the placement performs, not just that it closed.
