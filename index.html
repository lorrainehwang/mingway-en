About Us

A boutique firm built on long-term trust

Mingway Consulting is a boutique recruitment consultancy based in Shanghai, specialised in mid-to-senior level search and project-based recruitment partnerships. Through every successful delivery, we build lasting, trusted, growth-oriented relationships — with clients and candidates alike.

We work on a Key Account model: through high-quality, high-efficiency talent connections, we earn the long-term trust of our clients and become their partner in talent value, powering the needs of business growth. At the same time, we build enduring relationships with outstanding candidates — offering deep, professional service and walking alongside them as their careers develop. We grow together, and we succeed together.
